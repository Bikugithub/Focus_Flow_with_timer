Quick Guide to FocusFlow

Everything you need to run calm, distraction-free sessions:

  1. Pick a Mode & Label: Choose Focus, Short Break, or Long Break, then tap a label (like Study or Coding) and add an optional note.
  2. Start & Stay Calm: Hit START. The timer stays accurate even if you switch tabs or pages.
  3. Finish & Reflect: When time ends, a soft chime plays, a desktop alert triggers, and a motivational quote appears.
  4. Review Utilities: Open History to search/filter saved sessions by label, Analytics for 7-day charts, and Settings to customize minutes.
